Methods tried
-------------
1. Raw online best-effort LiDAR replay

   Result:
     Failed / unstable.

   Evidence:
     Earlier raw online tests produced LiDAR gap and points-vs-IMU time
     difference warnings even though direct metadata/header inspection showed
     the raw bag LiDAR header timestamps were continuous.

   Cause:
     The problem was not the MCAP timestamps themselves. The online ROS2
     best-effort replay/subscription path was dropping or delaying LiDAR frames
     enough for GLIM to see discontinuities.

2. Over-integrated adapter-owned sync

   Result:
     Rejected as the main direction.

   Cause:
     It made the adapter responsible for too much sensor scheduling policy and
     drifted away from the adapter_clean contract. It also created confusing
     behavior around deadlines and degraded/drop decisions.

3. Prepped-bag / D3 control

   Result:
     Useful as a control only.

   Evidence:
     The prepped/control path did not show the u-turn, proving the core GLIM
     mapping path can produce a sane trajectory when inputs are deterministic.

   Limitation:
     It is not the accepted final approach because it relies on a preprocessed
     bag and is not the requested online path.

4. Online adapter_clean-style path with reliable LiDAR QoS

   Result:
     Accepted.

   Key changes:
     - Keep adapter focused on Point One / GPS-P1 normalization.
     - Keep raw rosbag as the source of LiDAR and Atlas pose.
     - Use PCAP IMU replay through adapter for /gps_p1/imu.
     - Subscribe/play /luminar_front/points with reliable QoS and deep queue.
     - Keep GLIM concat disabled for this validation.
     - Use INS-driven GLIM odometry + CPU sub/global mapping config.
     - Use process-group shutdown so GLIM reliably receives SIGINT and saves.

   Why it succeeded:
     Reliable LiDAR QoS removed the hidden replay/subscription frame drops that
     previously caused GLIM LiDAR gaps and points/IMU time mismatch. Once the
     input stream was continuous, the online trajectory no longer showed the
     u-turn failure.

Remaining notes
